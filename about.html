Behind the Vision

A Sanctuary Carved Away From the Noise

Oxygen Hotel was conceived as a response to the fast-paced energy of modern life. Located in the heart of Abuja, our intent is simple: to create a tactile, peaceful haven where minimal design meets effortless warmth.

Every corner—from the rich textures of teak wood to our custom architectural lighting—has been thoughtfully calibrated to encourage stillness, presence, and restoration.
